              On this page

                About this prototype. Common Ground is a student design concept: a 2035 community for adults aged 60 and over that links everyday movement, meal planning and time with others. This planner was built with AI assistance for discussion. It has not been tested with real participants. People over 60 differ widely in fitness, food, budget, transport and how sociable they like to be, so every idea is optional.

                Educational planning only, not medical advice. In an emergency call 999. For urgent medical advice call 111 or go to 111.nhs.uk.

                What this site counts

                Your answers stay in your browser. They are never sent anywhere, and closing the page clears them.

                To improve the prototype we count anonymous events, such as how many people reach each step, finish a plan or press Print, and the answers to the optional feedback questions. We never send your age, sex, height, weight, health answers or plan.

                Counting uses GoatCounter, which doesn’t use cookies and doesn’t keep IP addresses. Along with each count it records only the country, the type of browser and device, and the time. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, nothing is counted.
